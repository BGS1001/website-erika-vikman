Biography

The Tango Queen
of Finland

Erika Susanna Vikman won Finland's tango crown, then took everything it taught her about holding a room and pointed it at the pop charts. Basel got the full version: a golden microphone, live fireworks, and nothing toned down.

The road

From Tampere to Basel.

1993

Born into it

Born on 20 February in Tampere, Finland, and raised between Lempäälä and Pori. Her mother was a tango singer who competed at Tangomarkkinat — Finland's storied tango festival — and her younger sister Jennika sings too.

2013

Idols, and a wildcard

She sang her way onto season seven of Finnish Idols in 2013, went out in ninth, came back through the wildcard round and finished seventh. The early lesson: getting sent home is not the same as being finished.

2016

Tango Queen of Finland

Runner-up at Tangomarkkinat in 2015. She came back in 2016 and won it outright at 23, taking the title that has followed her name ever since — through every latex catsuit that came after.

2020

Cicciolina, and the turn to pop

"Cicciolina" — named for Ilona Staller, the Hungarian-Italian performer who went on to sit in the Italian parliament — won the public vote at Uuden Musiikin Kilpailu with 99 points and still lost the contest. Finland settled the matter itself: number five, her first top-five hit. "Syntisten pöytä" went to number three and gave her a first number one on radio.

The same year she competes as "The Wasp" on Masked Singer Suomi, finishing third, and publicly comes out as bisexual.

2021

A number-one debut

Her self-titled debut arrived on 20 August 2021 and entered the Finnish albums chart at number one. The tango hall had built a pop star, and the pop star had not left the tango hall behind.

2025

Ich Komme — Basel

Written and produced by Christel and Jori Roosberg, released through Warner Music Finland and Mökkitie Records, "Ich Komme" won Uuden Musiikin Kilpailu on a 75% public / 25% jury split and went to number two in Finland. The German in the title is not a gimmick: she took German at school, on the strength of a teacher who played her Rammstein and Nina Hagen.

She qualified third from the second semi-final with 115 points, then finished 11th in the Grand Final with 196. She had said publicly that she expected to be asked to soften the staging. What went out live was a giant golden microphone hoisting her over the arena, throwing fireworks — a song about her own pleasure, performed without a single apology.

Off stage

Beyond the spotlight.

The EP "Erikavision" arrived in May 2025. The writing still moves between tango-rooted balladry and maximalist electropop, because she has never accepted that those are two different jobs. The latex, the spikes, the sky-high boots are the same argument in another medium — and the crowd makes it back, turning up show after show in matching "ICH" shirts.
