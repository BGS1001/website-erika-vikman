Biography

The Tango Queen
of Finland

Erika Susanna Vikman spent a decade earning her place in Finland's oldest musical tradition before she blew it open — and then took the result to Basel on a flaming golden microphone.

The road

From Tampere to Basel.

1993

Born into it

Born on 20 February in Tampere, Finland, and raised between Lempäälä and Pori. Her mother was a tango singer who competed at Tangomarkkinat — Finland's storied tango festival — and her younger sister Jennika sings too.

2013

Idols, and a wildcard

Her professional road starts on season seven of Finnish Idols, where a wildcard return carries her to seventh place.

2016

Tango Queen of Finland

Runner-up at Tangomarkkinat in 2015, she returns the following year and wins outright — taking the title that still follows her name.

2020

Cicciolina, and the turn to pop

"Cicciolina" — named after the Hungarian-Italian performer and politician Ilona Staller — places second at Uuden Musiikin Kilpailu but becomes her first top-five hit. "Syntisten pöytä" follows to number three and gives her a first number-one radio hit.

The same year she competes as "The Wasp" on Masked Singer Suomi, finishing third, and publicly comes out as bisexual.

2021

A number-one debut

Her self-titled debut album arrives in August and goes straight to number one in Finland — proof the tango hall had built a pop star made to last.

2025

Ich Komme — Basel

Written and produced by Christel and Jori Roosberg, released through Warner Music Finland and Mökkitie Records, "Ich Komme" wins Uuden Musiikin Kilpailu on a 75% public / 25% jury split and carries Finland to the Eurovision Song Contest.

She qualifies third from her semi-final with 115 points, then finishes 11th in the Grand Final with 196 — the performance defined by being hoisted skyward on a giant golden microphone stand, throwing fireworks over the arena.

Off stage

Beyond the spotlight.

Erika released the EP "Erikavision" in 2025 and keeps writing music that moves between tango-rooted balladry and maximalist electropop. She is known as much for her fearless personal style — latex, spikes, sky-high boots — as for her voice, and for a fanbase that turns up in matching "ICH" shirts.
