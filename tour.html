The experience

Not just a concert.

Since Eurovision 2025, an Erika Vikman show means pyrotechnics, a wall of sound, and — if you're lucky — the golden microphone rig making an appearance. Expect a crowd in matching "Ich Komme" merch, confetti, and a setlist that swings from tango balladry to full-throttle electropop.
