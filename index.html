The story

From Tango­markkinat
to Basel.

Born in Tampere in 1993, raised between Lempäälä and Pori, Erika Vikman won Finland's tango crown at 23 — then spent the next decade proving it was never a costume. "Cicciolina" carried the tango hall onto the pop charts. "Ich Komme" carried it to Basel.

  • 2016 Wins Tangomarkkinat and is crowned Tango Queen of Finland
  • 2020 "Cicciolina" wins the UMK public vote, loses the contest, and charts anyway
  • 2025 Wins UMK with "Ich Komme" and takes Finland to Basel

Eurovision 2025 · Basel

The golden microphone moment.

She said she expected to be asked to tone the staging down. What Basel got instead: hoisted skyward on a giant golden microphone, throwing fireworks over the arena. Eleventh in the Grand Final on 196 points, and the Eurovision Awards named her Style Icon 2025.
