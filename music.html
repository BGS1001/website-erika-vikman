Eurovision 2025 entry

Ich Komme

Written and produced by Christel and Jori Roosberg, released through Warner Music Finland and Mökkitie Records. It won Uuden Musiikin Kilpailu on a 75/25 public-jury split, went to number two in Finland, and took her to Basel.
